# LinkedIn Post #2 — Junior Task Watch #1: Analyst (Tue 2026-11-24)

## Post copy

A junior analyst's day, task by task.

10 tasks. 10 hours.

65% is already copilot-touched. By 2030, 65% is agent-executable.

The top of the chart: summarising earnings releases, 90%; pulling and cleaning data, 85%; compliance checks, 80%; updating comps models, 75%.

Those are not random tasks. They are the apprenticeship.

RBC analysts cover 17.5+ companies each. QuickTakes drafts research 95% faster.

At Anthropic, the junior share of technical hires fell from 27% to 9%.

I score each task on capability, observed use, and agent autonomy by 2030. That is the Agentic Exposure Index.

The human core that remains, judgment and relationships, is 35% of the task content. It carries almost all of the training value.

If two-thirds of the junior day becomes agent work, where do senior analysts come from in 2035?

—
Method note: Task taxonomy from O*NET (financial analysts). Scores combine the Anthropic Economic Index with bank disclosures (RBC, CIBC). Dataset and code are published in this repository — challenge my scores, that's the point. Independent research, not investment advice.
