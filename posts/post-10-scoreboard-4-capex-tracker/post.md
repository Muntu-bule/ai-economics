# LinkedIn Post #10 — Scoreboard #4: The AI Capex Tracker (Wed 2026-12-02)

## Post copy

The money is going in anyway.

JPMorgan's technology budget is about $18 billion a year. Its AI spend is nearer $700 million. The budget is twenty-six times the AI spend.

Bank of America spends about $13 billion on technology and expects about $700 million of AI benefit next year. That is under 1% of its $74 billion cost base.

Wells Fargo budgets above $10 billion. RBC spends about C$6 billion.

TD, BMO, Scotiabank, CIBC, Citigroup and Capital One publish no comparable total. The gaps are listed, not charted, and never drawn as zeros.

Technology spend is not AI spend. No bank breaks AI out cleanly.

Yesterday's scoreboard showed the numerator: 12 banks putting a number on the value. This is the denominator. The ratio is the question.

Is this investment, or overinvestment?
