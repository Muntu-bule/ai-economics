# LinkedIn Post #6 — Scoreboard #2: The US Edition (Sat 2026-11-28)

## Post copy

Eight of the top 10 are North American.

Evident's 2026 AI Index ranks 50 banks. Eight of the top ten are headquartered in North America: six American, two Canadian. In 2023 it was six.

The top three never moved.

Below them, the order shuffled. TD 13 to 10. Bank of America 10 to 7. Citigroup 12 to 8. Morgan Stanley 5 to 9.

The average bank score rose 26%. The gap between first and tenth narrowed by a quarter.

Only 12 of the 50 banks report any realised or projected AI value. Only 12% of 1,107 use cases disclose a KPI effect. Barely 1% disclose a financial return.

The race is now about who can show the value, not who adopted first.

Adoption alone is no longer the differentiator. So what is?
