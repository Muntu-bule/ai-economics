# LinkedIn Post #9 — Scoreboard #3: The Returns Gap (Tue 2026-12-01)

## Post copy

1,107 use cases, 12 with a number.

Only 12% disclose an effect on an operational KPI. Barely 1% disclose a financial return. Only 12 of the 50 report any realised or projected AI value, up from 8.

RBC targets $700 million in net AI value by next year, toward C$700M–$1B by fiscal 2027. TD reported C$195 million in nine months, against a C$200 million target. That is 97.5% of the way there.

They are value reported, not returns earned. Nobody has published the spend underneath, so no return can be computed.

MIT found 5% of AI pilots show measurable P&L impact. The average bank score rose 26%, and the gap between #1 and #10 narrowed by a quarter.
