# Synthesis — "Three Occupations, One Pattern" (AEI public launch)

## Post copy

Three occupations. One pattern.

Junior analysts: 65% of the task day agent-executable by 2030. Contact centre: 70%. Junior developers: 72%.

That is not three findings. It is one finding, three times. Today, the index behind it.

The Agentic Exposure Index scores each task on capability, observed use, and agent autonomy by 2030. The headline is autonomy: the share of task hours an agent could execute.

The tasks at the top are the ones where correctness is cheapest to verify: summarising releases, 90%; after-call summaries, 90%; boilerplate code, 90%.

What remains is the human core: judgment and relationships, a third of the analyst's day; empathy under pressure; design and translation, one hour out of eight.

It describes tasks, not employment. Nothing here is causal.

Where does the work go, and what remains human?

Method note: Sources — Anthropic Economic Index; O*NET task taxonomy; bank disclosures. Dataset and code are published in this repository. Independent research, not investment advice.
