# LinkedIn Post #1 — Big 5 AI Scoreboard


Every Big 5 bank is top 30.

RBC is #3 in the world. TD cracked the top 10.

But only 12 of the 50 banks report any realised or projected AI value.

Adoption is world-class. Disclosure of value is not.

Every Big 5 bank runs copilots: RBC, 72,000+ on AI tools; TD, C$195M reported in nine months; BMO, 90%+ of 53,000 on Copilot; CIBC, 50,000+ on CAI; Scotiabank on Google Cloud.

Copilots eat tasks. Agents eat workflows.

The tasks most exposed first are the ones junior analysts have done.

Where does the value show up: revenue, costs, or headcount?

—
Method note: Sources — Evident AI Index 2026 (Oct 2026 edition); bank disclosures and earnings calls 2025–26. Figures are reported AI value, not returns — no spend denominator is disclosed. Analysis code is published in this repository. This is independent research, not investment advice.
