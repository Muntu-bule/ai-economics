# LinkedIn Post #5 — Junior Task Watch #3: Developer (Fri 2026-11-27)

## Post copy

The honest data is not from a bank.

It comes from Anthropic's own Institute.

The junior share of its technical hires fell from 27% to 9%. Commits rose 5x. Agent hours passed human hours in March, and hit 6:1 by August.

A junior developer's day in banking: 72% is agent-executable by 2030.

Boilerplate code, 90%. Documentation, 90%. Unit tests, 85%. Routine bug fixes, 80%. Well-specified feature tickets, 70%.

Agents take over fastest where correctness is cheapest to verify. Code compiles or it does not. Tests pass or they fail.

What remains human-core: system design trade-offs, and understanding what the business needs. One hour out of eight.

If the company that understands this technology best cut its junior hiring share by two-thirds, what should a first-year computer science student do differently this year?
