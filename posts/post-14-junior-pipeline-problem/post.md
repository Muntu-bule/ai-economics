## Post copy (paste as-is; chart image attached) — 132 words

Three occupations, one pattern.

Junior analysts: 65% of the task day agent-executable by 2030. Contact centre: 70%. Junior developers: 72%.

Read the three charts and the pattern is hard to miss. The tasks at the top are the apprenticeship: summarising releases, after-call notes, boilerplate.

Anthropic hired two-thirds fewer juniors into technical roles: 27% of technical hires were junior, now 9%.

RBC analysts cover 17.5+ companies. CIBC's voice assistant handled 16M+ calls.

AI-exposed workers are 16 percentage points more likely to be women. The contact centre is banking's first rung.

Agents take over fastest where correctness is cheapest to verify. The question is whether the apprenticeship is redesigned around what agents cannot verify, or left to shrink.

If two-thirds of the junior day becomes agent work, where do seniors come from in 2035?
