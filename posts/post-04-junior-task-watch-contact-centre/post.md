# LinkedIn Post #4 — Junior Task Watch #2: Contact Centre (Thu 2026-11-26)

## Post copy

Banking's real first rung.

The contact centre: 9 tasks, a 7.5-hour shift, 70% agent-executable by 2030.

Routine balance inquiries, 90%. After-call summaries, 90%. Simple service requests, 85%. Identity verification, 80%.

CIBC's voice assistant has handled 16M+ calls. TD has piloted generative AI in its contact centres.

There are a few hundred junior analyst seats in Canadian banking. There are tens of thousands of contact-centre seats.

AI-exposed workers are 16 percentage points more likely to be women. The distribution story is here, not on the trading floor.

What remains: de-escalation, fraud victims, hardship, bereavement. Empathy under pressure, the one thing clients can tell is fake when automated.

This is occupation two in the Agentic Exposure Index.

When the first rung becomes an AI agent, where do people start, and what replaces the rung?

—
Method note: Task taxonomy from O*NET (customer service representatives). Scores combine the Anthropic Economic Index with bank disclosures (CIBC, TD). Dataset and code are published in this repository. Independent research, not investment advice.
